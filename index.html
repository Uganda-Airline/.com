    About UAC

    Uganda Airlines Corporation (UAC) handles the booking of flights to various destinations across the world. We also operate short-distance flights tailored for businesses and organizations.

    Together with the Uganda Tourism Board, we promote tourist activities in Uganda, from national parks and geographical scenes to museums and culture, as well as luxurious hotels in Uganda and abroad.
